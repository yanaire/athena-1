Purpose
Verify the frequency response of an ideal RC circuit and compare its cutoff frequency with the analytical value.
Method
A 1 kΩ resistor and a 100 nF capacitor were used. The AC response was analyzed from 10 Hz to 100 kHz.
Results
This is within the predefined 1% tolerance.
Scope and limitations
This analysis excludes real component tolerances, non-ideal behavior, and measurement equipment effects.
